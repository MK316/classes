## 📝 Assignment 1. Final Reflection

**Length: Approximately 1 page**

Throughout the semester, we have read and discussed research articles, practiced academic presentations, and explored how academic research and publishing work.

Take this opportunity to reflect on your experiences using the following three questions.

### 1. What changed?
- What did you learn or find interesting this semester?
- Has anything changed in how you read research articles or think about academic research?

### 2. What remains challenging?
- What aspects of reading, presenting, discussing, or conducting research do you still find difficult?
- Is there anything you would like to learn more about?

### 3. What's next?
- Is there a topic or question related to your teaching experience that you would like to explore further? Summarize your idea and write 2-3 research questions in the report.
- How might you apply what you learned in this seminar to your teaching or professional development?

### ✏️ Writing Guidelines

- Write approximately **one page**.
- Use your own experiences and examples from the seminar.
- You do not need to answer every sub-question.
- Focus on personal reflections rather than summarizing course content.

## 📄 Assignment 2. Response to Reviewers

### 🎯 Purpose

This assignment provides an opportunity to experience an important part of academic publishing: **responding to peer-review comments from the author's perspective**.

### 📚 Materials Provided

You will receive:

1. A sample research manuscript (Google drive; password required)
2. Reviewers' comments on the manuscript (Google drive; password required) 

### ✅ Your Task

Imagine that you are the author of the manuscript and have received feedback from journal reviewers.

Prepare a **Response to Reviewers** document following these steps:

1. Read the sample manuscript and reviewers' comments carefully.
2. Identify the main concerns raised by the reviewers.
3. Write a **point-by-point response** to the comments.
4. Explain how you would address each concern through revisions.
5. If you disagree with a comment, provide a reasonable academic justification.

> **Important:** You do not need to revise and resubmit the entire manuscript. Your task is to explain how you would respond to the reviewers and what revisions you would make.


### ✏️ Writing Guidelines

- Respond to the reviewer's comments **one by one**.
- Maintain a professional and respectful tone.
- Explain your reasoning clearly and specifically.
- You may respectfully disagree with a reviewer when justified.
- Focus on the quality of your responses, not the length of the document.

> **Note:** Since you are proposing revisions rather than actually revising the manuscript, expressions such as *"We would revise..."* or *"We would clarify..."* are appropriate.
